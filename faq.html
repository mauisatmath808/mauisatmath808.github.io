FAQ

Questions parents ask

Don't see yours? Email me at mauisatmath@gmail.com.

Who is the tutor?

Me, Noah. I'm a student in Makawao, and I got a 5 on the AP Calculus AB exam. You can read more on the About page.

What subjects do you tutor?

Algebra 1, Geometry, Algebra 2, Trig, Precalc, Calc AB, and SAT math. I don't tutor Calc BC, college stats, or anything past Calc AB.

How much does it cost?

$35 an hour for school math and $40 an hour for SAT math. You can also get 4 sessions for $120 (school math) or $140 (SAT math), which saves $20. In person sessions are $10 more.

Is the first session free?

The first 15 minutes of the first session are free. If it isn't a good fit, you don't pay anything.

How do I pay?

By Venmo. I send a request after the free 15 minutes. Packs are paid up front.

How do I book?

Pick a time on the booking page. I'm available Monday to Friday from 3:30 to 6:30 pm and weekends from 9 am to 6 pm, Hawaii time.

Online or in person?

Either one. Online sessions are on Google Meet, and the link comes with your calendar invite. In person works in Makawao, Pukalani, Haiku, Kula, and Paia.

What do we need for an online session?

A laptop or tablet with a camera and microphone, the homework or practice test you want to work on, and Desmos open at desmos.com/calculator.

Can parents sit in?

Yes, parents are always welcome to sit in on any session.

What if we need to cancel?

Just cancel at least 12 hours ahead. Later than that, the session counts.

How many sessions will my student need?

It depends on the student and the goal. After the first session, I'll tell you what I think and about how many sessions it should take. A 4 pack is a good way to start.

Do you help with homework or just tests?

Both. School math sessions usually start with whatever is due next, and then we work on the gaps behind it.
