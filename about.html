About

Hi, I'm Noah.

I'm a student in Makawao, and I got a 5 on the AP Calculus AB exam. I started Maui SAT Math to help other students with the same math I just went through, from Algebra 1 all the way up to Calc AB.

I know what it's like to learn this material right now, as I was sitting in these classes very recently. I remember which parts were confusing, and I explain things the way I wish someone had explained them to me.

SAT math is my specialty. The digital SAT has the Desmos calculator built in, and a lot of students don't know how much time it can save. I also focus on the question types that come up over and over, so students stop losing pretty easy points.

Sessions are online over Google Meet, or in person around Upcountry. Parents are always welcome to sit in.
